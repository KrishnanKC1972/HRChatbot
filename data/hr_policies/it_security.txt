**IT Security Policy**

1. Password Requirements
- Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and contain uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols.
- Passwords must be changed every 90 days.

2. Device Security
- All company devices must have antivirus software installed.
- Employees must report lost or stolen devices immediately.

3. Data Protection
- Sensitive data must be encrypted when transmitted or stored.
- Access to sensitive systems requires multi-factor authentication.
