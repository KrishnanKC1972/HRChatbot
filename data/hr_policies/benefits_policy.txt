**Benefits Policy**

1. Health Insurance
- Company provides comprehensive health insurance for full-time employees and their dependents.
- Coverage begins 30 days after employment start date.

2. Retirement Plan
- 401(k) plan with 4% company matching is available to all eligible employees.
- Employees can enroll after 90 days of employment.

3. Professional Development
- Employees are eligible for $2000 annual budget for training and development.
- Courses must be job-related and approved by manager.
