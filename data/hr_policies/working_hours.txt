**Working Hours Policy**

1. Standard Hours
- Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday.
- Employees are entitled to a 1-hour lunch break.
- Flexible working arrangements may be approved by department managers.

2. Overtime
- Overtime must be pre-approved by a manager.
- Eligible overtime hours are compensated at 1.5x the regular hourly rate.

3. Remote Work
- Hybrid work is available with manager approval.
- Employees working remotely must maintain a safe and productive workspace.
