**Leave Policy**

1. Annual Leave
- Full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- Annual leave accrues monthly at a rate of 1.67 days per month.
- Unused annual leave can be carried over to the next year, up to a maximum of 40 days.
- Leave requests must be submitted at least 14 days in advance for planned vacations.

2. Sick Leave
- Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year.
- Medical certification may be required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

3. Parental Leave
- Eligible employees can take up to 12 weeks of unpaid parental leave within the first year of a child's birth or adoption.
