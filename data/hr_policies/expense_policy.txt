**Expense Policy**

1. Travel Expenses
- Employees must use approved travel providers when booking flights and hotels.
- Receipts must be submitted for all expenses over $25.

2. Meal Allowances
- Daily meal allowance is $40 for business travel within the US.
- Alcohol is not reimbursable.

3. Approval Process
- All expenses must be approved by manager prior to purchase.
- Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of incurring expense.
