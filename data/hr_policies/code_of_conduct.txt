**Code of Conduct**

1. Professional Behavior
- Employees are expected to treat all colleagues with respect and professionalism.
- Discrimination, harassment, or bullying of any kind is strictly prohibited.

2. Confidentiality
- All company information, including client data, must be kept strictly confidential.
- Unauthorized disclosure may result in disciplinary action.

3. Conflict of Interest
- Employees must disclose any potential conflicts of interest to their manager.
- Gifts from vendors must not exceed $50 in value.
